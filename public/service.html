1. Our service

We arrange diagnostic tests with sample collection at your home. After you book and pay, we assign a partner collection centre. That centre sends a trained phlebotomist to collect your sample. The sample is tested and the report is sent to you on WhatsApp. You can also view it on our report portal.

Service depends on your PIN code, slot availability and the centres available near you.

2. Not medical advice

Test results support a consultation with a qualified doctor. They do not replace one.

Results must be interpreted by a registered medical practitioner, together with your symptoms, history and examination. Reference ranges can vary with age, sex, cycle day, medicines and the method used.

Do not start, stop or change any treatment based only on a report. In a medical emergency, contact a hospital or call 112.

3. Booking & eligibility

  • You must be 18 or older to book. A parent or guardian must book for anyone younger.
  • You may book for someone else only with their permission. You must enter their correct details.

  • A slot is held for a short time while you pay. If payment is not received in that time, the booking expires and the slot is released.
  • A booking is confirmed only after we verify your payment and message you on WhatsApp.

5. Home sample collection

  • Time slots are the window in which the collector aims to arrive. Traffic, weather or other events beyond our control may cause delays. If that happens we will keep you informed.
  • The collector's name and contact number will be sent to you on WhatsApp before your visit.
  • An adult must be present at the address. Please keep a valid ID ready if asked.
  • If the collector cannot reach you or collect the sample, we will offer another slot. Reasons include no one being home, a wrong address, or not following the preparation instructions.
  • Collectors follow standard safety and hygiene practices. Please tell them about any bleeding disorder, fainting history or medication that affects blood clotting.
  • Collection centres are independent partners. They work under our standard procedures.

6. Preparing for your test

Some tests need preparation, such as fasting for 10–12 hours, a particular day of your menstrual cycle, or a specific time of day. We send these instructions when you book, and they are also listed with each test.

If you do not follow them, the results may be inaccurate. In that case we may need to reschedule.

7. Rescheduling & cancellation

  • You can reschedule for free on WhatsApp or through our team, subject to slot availability. Please ask at least 3 hours before your slot.
  • You can cancel a confirmed booking any time before the collector leaves for your address.
  • We may cancel or reschedule if:
    • no collector is available in your area,
    • the address is outside our service area,
    • a test becomes unavailable, or
    • something beyond our control prevents collection.
    In these cases you can choose a new slot or a full refund.

8. Refunds

  • You cancel before the collector leaves: full refund.
  • We cancel, or we cannot collect for reasons on our side: full refund.
  • After the sample has been collected: no refund, because the test has been processed.
  • A sample is rejected by the lab, for example because it clotted or was not enough: we will re-collect it free of charge.
  • The collector visits but cannot collect because of reasons on your side: for example no one is home, a wrong address, or the preparation instructions were not followed. We will offer one free reschedule. After that, a visit charge may be deducted from any refund.

Approved refunds are made to the original UPI account within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take extra time to show it.
