Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Dr. Sarwan Diagnostic Labs offers diagnostic tests with home sample collection. This policy explains what happens if you want to change a booking or ask for your money back.

1. Cancellation

  • Bookings cannot be cancelled. Instead, you can reschedule your visit to another date or time, subject to slot availability.
  • To reschedule, message us on WhatsApp or call 8368143677, at least 1 hour before your slot.

2. Refunds

If you ask for your money back, the refund depends on whether the sample was collected:

  • Sample already collected: 90% of the amount paid is refunded. 10% of the fee is deducted for the visit and sample handling.
  • Sample not collected, and you told us at least 1 hour before your slot: 95% of the amount paid is refunded. 5% of the fee is deducted.
  • We cancel, for example because no collector is available, the address is outside our service area or a test is unavailable: full refund, or a new slot if you prefer.
  • Sample rejected by the lab (for example it clotted or was not enough): we re-collect it free of charge.

3. How refunds are paid

  • Ask for a refund on WhatsApp or by phone, with your booking reference (it starts with DSL-).
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI account) within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take extra time to show it.
