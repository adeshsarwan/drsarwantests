Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Dr. Sarwan Diagnostic Labs ("DSL", "we", "us") offers diagnostic tests with home sample collection through drsarwantests.com, WhatsApp and our partner collection centres. This policy explains what information we collect, why we need it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011. By booking a test or using our website or WhatsApp service, you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

Information you give us when you book:

  • Your name, mobile (WhatsApp) number and date of birth.
  • Your address for sample collection: house or flat, area, city, state and PIN code.
  • Health-related details you choose to share, such as the day of your menstrual cycle and any notes for the collector.
  • Payment details: the UPI transaction reference (UTR) and the payment screenshot you upload. We do not receive or store your card, bank account or UPI PIN.

Information created while we serve you:

  • Booking details: the test, date, time slot, booking status, assigned collection centre and collector.
  • Sample details, such as the vial number.
  • Your test reports.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, including photos or documents.
  • Basic technical information when you visit the website, such as your IP address and browser type. Our hosting provider keeps this to run and protect the service.

Test results, health details and the information above count as sensitive personal data. We treat them that way.

2. How we use it

  • To confirm your booking and send a collector to your address at the chosen time.
  • To check your payment.
  • To label and track your sample, run the tests and deliver your report.
  • To send booking updates, reminders, preparation instructions and reports. We send these on WhatsApp, or by phone if needed.
  • To answer your questions and handle reschedules, refunds and complaints.
  • To verify it is you before sharing a report. We use your mobile number together with your date of birth.
  • To meet legal, accounting and medical record-keeping duties, and to prevent fraud or misuse.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your health information for advertising.

3. WhatsApp messages

When you book, you agree to receive booking updates and reports from us on WhatsApp. We use the official WhatsApp Business Platform, run by Meta Platforms.

Messages are protected in transit by WhatsApp. Meta processes them under its own WhatsApp Business terms and privacy policy.

We only message you about bookings you have made. You can stop non-essential messages at any time by replying STOP or by telling our team. We may still need to contact you about an active booking or a report.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what each party needs to do its job:

  • Partner collection centres and their phlebotomists: your name, phone number, address, time slot and test, so they can collect your sample. A centre may also upload your report to us.
  • Testing laboratories: sample and test details needed to process your sample.
  • Service providers that run our systems:
    • Cloudflare: website hosting and database.
    • Meta: WhatsApp messaging.
    • Google: address search on the booking form.
    • Your UPI or payment app: payment.
    They process data on our instructions or under their own policies.
  • Your doctor or anyone you authorise, but only when you ask us to.
  • Government or law enforcement when the law requires it, for example notifiable disease reporting, a court order or a lawful request.

Some providers may store data on servers outside India. Where this happens, we rely on providers with strong security and contractual safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

  • Bookings and reports: kept for as long as needed to serve you and to meet medical record-keeping and tax rules. This is usually up to 3 years from your last booking, unless the law requires longer.
  • Bookings that are started but never paid: these expire automatically. We delete them periodically.
  • Payment screenshots and WhatsApp conversations: kept only as long as needed to check payments and resolve queries.

6. Security

  • All traffic to our website uses HTTPS.
  • Reports can be opened only with a private link, or after confirming your mobile number and date of birth.
  • Staff and collection-centre logins use individual accounts with hashed passwords. Repeated failed logins are locked out.
  • Access is limited to people who need it to serve you.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Withdraw your consent. This won't affect anything we did before you withdrew it. Without some data we may not be able to provide a test.
  • Ask us to delete your data. We may keep some records where the law requires it.
  • Name someone to act for you if you die or become unable to act.
  • Raise a grievance with us and, if you are not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 12.

8. Deleting your data

To have your data deleted:

  1. Send us a WhatsApp message or email from your registered mobile number, saying "Delete my data". Use the contact details in section 12.
  2. We will confirm who you are, usually by asking for your date of birth.
  3. We will delete your bookings, reports, messages and payment proofs within 30 days, and tell you when it is done.

We may keep invoices and the minimum records the law requires, and we will tell you if we do.

9. Children

Bookings for anyone under 18 must be made by a parent or legal guardian, who gives consent on the child's behalf. We do not knowingly collect a child's data without that consent.

10. Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our staff, centre and patient portals use small session tokens stored in your browser to keep you signed in. Our hosting provider may set strictly necessary security cookies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make significant changes, we will post a notice on the website or tell you on WhatsApp.
