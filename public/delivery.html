Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Dr. Sarwan Diagnostic Labs provides a service, not physical products. Nothing is shipped to you.

1. Home sample collection

  • After your payment is confirmed, our collector visits the address you gave, on the date and in the time slot you booked.
  • You receive the booking confirmation and the collector's details on WhatsApp. The collector calls before arriving.
  • Home collection is available in our service area. If we can't serve your address, we tell you and offer a full refund (see our Refund & Cancellation Policy).

2. Report delivery – online only

  • Your reports are delivered online: we send them to you on WhatsApp, and they are always available at drsarwantests.com/reports (sign in with your mobile number and date of birth).
  • When the report will be ready depends on the tests booked. We message you on WhatsApp as soon as it is ready.
  • No printed reports are posted or couriered.
